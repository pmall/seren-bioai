What's on the radar

  • Sanofi pays Regeneron $1B to extend the Dupixent franchise inward

    $1B upfront plus up to $7B milestones for four long-acting IL-4/13 antibodies, lead REGN20423 anti-IL-13 in Phase 1, plus a lunsekimig opt-in; 50-50 split, audit suit settled. After amlitelimab and itepekimab were axed in July — the successor map narrows to the originator.

  • Survodutide Phase 3 hits but misses the new bar

    Up to 9.8% loss on the regimen estimand, 13.1% on efficacy, vs 3.9% placebo at 76 weeks; ~18% GI discontinuation; shares −8%, second "less competitive" call against Lilly's 23.3% amylin combo. Glucagon duals pushed to the quality-of-loss niche.

  • Merck anti-TL1A posts 72% response in hidradenitis suppurativa

    Tulisokibart Phase 2b: 72% high-dose and 64% medium HiSCR50 vs +37/+29 points over placebo, every-4-week dosing, no serious infections; from the $10.8B Prometheus buy. Efficacy plus interval plus clean infections is the I&I bundle.

    Limited liver events including one blinded death in admilparant; protocol amended, independent committee says continue; readthrough briefly hit Contineum's PIPE-791. Mechanism-vs-molecule question into the Q4 ALOFT-IPF catalyst.

    Solubility-fixed niclosamide targets lesion cells in mice; 190M affected, ~50% surgical recurrence. Preclinical repurpose-plus-formulation path for a missed disease.

  • Lilly EloraTZP posts 23.3% weight loss in Phase 2b
