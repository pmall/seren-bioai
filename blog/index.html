What's on the radar

  • ADARx $446M IPO leads a Financing wave: City, Iambic, TRex, Retension file

  • HBV epigenetic editing reaches the clinic without cutting DNA

    nChroma CRMA-1001 uses dead-Cas9 methylation to silence cccDNA plus integrated HBV; mice and liver cells promising, monkeys clean, first IV dose in January. Non-cut durable silencing for 250M+ chronic patients.

  • iLands: 70,000 persistent agents spamming researchers

    No-code agents soliciting data, donations, and paid work — 50+ emails a week to one researcher, no successful collaborations known. The trust-governance flip side of co-scientist maturation.

  • OpenAI research chief calls a "cultural reset" after hacks and breach

    Model-monitors-model chain-of-thought, Codex as "research intern" at 100k-chip scale, 2028 end-to-end goal bottlenecked on "research taste"; drug discovery named as shrink-the-pipeline target.

  • China deep-dive puts numbers on structural supply

    35 deals/$989M in 2019 to 186/$137.7B in 2025; Chinese assets over two-thirds of 2026 deal value vs under 5% five years ago; 20B+ RMB state spend in 2023. Discover in China, sell globally.

  • ART follow-up urges caution: intriguing array, unknown function

    950 agents over 21 hours, repeat array next to odd RT in jumbo phage, short-RNA expression, no known DNA-cutting partner; preprint, not peer-reviewed. Characterization-to-tool gap is the test.

  • Kyverna miv-cel holds at one year in stiff person syndrome

    49% mobility gain, 67% off walking aids, no high-grade CRS/ICANS/IEC-HS in n=26, rolling BLA 2026. Counterpoint to the Novartis/BMS autoimmune CAR-T pauses — safety splitting by construct, not class failure.

  • Roche and Lilly stack platform deals: Atavistik $2B, InnoCare $3.35B

    Roche–Atavistik allosteric CVRM small molecules ($70M upfront) plus Lilly–InnoCare five-target discovery pact ($100M upfront). China-originated platforms now structural supply for US pharma; Lilly's 2026 spree passes $25B.

  • Basecamp raises $140M with NVIDIA and Anthropic backing

    EDEN on the Trillion Gene Atlas, first focus in vivo cell therapies. Tech-lab capital directly funding the AI-native pipeline, alongside Enveda's $311M Series E.

  • AlphaFold goes viral: 8,028 dimers across 23 human-infecting families

    SIB re-annotation of 41,774 proteins fixes the polyprotein-cleavage blind spot; all predictions public. Open infrastructure for entry-protein and antibody work — no glycans or trimers yet.

  • Shokat lab posts bitopic kinase design rules

    PonatiLink-2 matches/beats ponatinib on compound resistance mutants with a wider window in mice; cooperativity plus linker entropy as the recipe. Avidity chemistry beyond PROTACs.

  • Insilico launches Longevity Vaccines: circular-mRNA plus targeted LNP to clear aging drivers

    In vivo T-cell engineering against senescent lymphocytes first, via PandaOmics plus Chemistry42 plus inClinico and a Virtual Aging Cell stack — extending the rentosertib dual-purpose aging strategy after six proteomic clocks showed age reversal. Nine candidates in eight months of 2026; initiative announcement, no clinical data yet.

  • Cradle–Lundbeck loop posts deployment metrics

    Two CNS antibodies end-to-end, 12k hours/week savings, months to under 24h, 50,000x searchable space, with Bayer, Novo Nordisk, and J&J on the same platform — mid-size pharma embedding AI protein engineering as infrastructure, not a vendor pilot.

  • Inductive Bio launches Indy, a medicinal-chemistry agent that reads the curves

    CRO parsing plus dose-response QC plus SAR plus FEP plus slides, with doubled team productivity claimed and 89% vs 39% (GPT) vs 48% (Claude) on 84 real dose-response curves. Live use at Anvia — agentic chemistry with head-to-head numbers, not just a pilot.

  • QuantaMind claims reactive modeling at DFT accuracy and 100k-atom scale

    Transition-state reactive MLFF in Science Advances: tens-of-ns reactive MD on 10k-atom systems, 0.25s per 100k-atom step claimed, full enzyme-cycle framing, plus a pH-sensitive antibody with 62x dissociation shift at pH 6.0 vs 7.4. Peer-reviewed venue, press-release numbers — physics-first counterpoint to pooled-structure empiricism.

  • Rentosertib reverses biological age in IPF patients

    Six proteomic aging clocks from Harvard, Oxford, PKU, and Insilico unanimously show rentosertib reversed biological age in 42 IPF patients — peak effect ~3-4 years at Week 4 (30 mg BID), up to 6 years in one clock.
