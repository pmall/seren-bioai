# Report — 2026-09-21

## Findings

- **2026-09-17** — Stanford "Virtual Biotech" published in *Science*: 37,000 AI agents organized like a biotech company (CSO directing division employees), powered by Claude, analyzed 55,000+ clinical trials. Key finding: drugs targeting proteins active in specific cell types were ~50% more likely to reach market. With human oversight, identified CD276 as a promising lung-cancer target — proposed ADC strategy (CD276 antibody tethered to anticancer drug). Not validated experimentally yet. Led by James Zou, Stanford. Signals: agentic AI is scaling from single-task tools to organizational structures that mirror real drug companies — the "virtual biotech" pattern is a step beyond Pathos Foundry's thousands of agents. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02954-y / https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aeg6779

- **2026-09-17** — Insilico rentosertib ageing clocks analysis reported in *Nature*: six proteomic clocks showed age reversal in IPF patients on rentosertib, but researchers struggled to conclude whether the drug truly reversed fundamental ageing processes or merely improved overall health. The "where does ageing end, disease start?" question remains unsolved. Zhavoronkov et al. published a companion *Cell* paper with an AI system (LLMs trained on ageing-biology data + 17 benchmark tasks + agent interface) to turbocharge longevity research. Signals: the longevity-AI intersection is maturing — the field now has dedicated LLM benchmarks for ageing research, but the fundamental interpretive problem (biological age vs. disease improvement) persists. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02913-7 / https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.026

- **2026-09-18** — OpenAI's most advanced model proved that Navier-Stokes equations produce singularities (infinite speeds) in certain vortex-stretching scenarios — solving a Millennium Prize Problem. The singularity appears at ~70nm width for air, where the continuum assumption breaks down. Implications for drug discovery: Navier-Stokes governs fluid flow in microfluidics, drug delivery, and biological systems at molecular scales. The AI-math connection may eventually improve molecular dynamics simulations used in computational drug design. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02922-6

- **2026-09-18** — Anthropic partners with Accenture (via Faculty, Accenture's AI business) on embedded evaluation: independent evaluators will work inside Anthropic with employee-level access to watch models during training, verify safety commitments, identify blind spots, and report incidents publicly. Each party investing at least $1B over 5 years. Non-exclusive; also in dialogue with METR. Signals: the "embedded evaluator" model is a new category of AI safety infrastructure — independent oversight embedded inside frontier labs, not just reviewing outputs. This could become the template for how life-sciences AI companies demonstrate compliance with biosecurity requirements. https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation

- **2026-09-17** — Roche bets up to $1B with Dualitas Therapeutics on bispecific antibodies for immunology/inflammation. Upfront $36.5M. Dualitas' DualScreen platform screens 300,000+ bispecific combinations, discovering target pairs from biology rather than pre-determining them. Roche handles preclinical through commercial. Follows Roche's Oct 2025 Qyuns ($75M upfront, TSLP/IL-33), Aug 2026 Hanmi ($2.3B obesity), DualityBio ($1B cancer ADC), Jun 2026 Nurix ($2.3B protein degrader). Signals: Roche's bispecific franchise (Columvi, Lunsumio, Vabysmo) is being extended through platform deals — DualScreen's "discover the biology" approach contrasts with pre-specified target pairs. https://www.biospace.com/deals/roche-bets-up-to-1b-to-advance-next-gen-bispecifics-with-dualitas
